# Sign-in photographs

The sign-in page shows a photograph behind each of its four slides when one is
here, named `slide-1.jpg` to `slide-4.jpg`. None ships with the code, because a
photo needs a licence the organisation holds; without them the slides show the
brand gradient.

Use landscape photos of at least 1600 x 1000 pixels, under about 400 KB each. Keep
the subject to the right: the text sits on the left, over a dark shade.
